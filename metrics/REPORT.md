Findings, in order of weight:
- `jobs.classifySubmit` tested `errors.Is(err, provider.ErrAccess)` before the `EDGE_BLOCKED` code, and both backends' `EDGE_BLOCKED` errors satisfy `ErrAccess`, so a Cloudflare 1010 failed the job `PROVIDER_AUTH` instead of `EDGE_BLOCKED` (the unit tests used an error shape that is not an `ErrAccess`). Fixed in `internal/jobs/submit.go` (one reordering); `TestChaos_SubmitErrors*` pin it on both backends.
- A batch whose activity crosses a calendar-month end can never be reconciled: C76.5 step 4 forbids a window that crosses the month and requires every batch created before `B` to be quiet at `B`, so at the month boundary `B` never qualifies and the window start never advances; the batch, and every later one, stays outstanding (conservative for the caps, and the stalled push names it after 72 h). Pinned by `TestBilling_BatchStraddlingMonthEndWaits`; the property suite skips its end-state assertions for such a timeline. Recorded as the open decision D-M3-37 in the plan and a **blocker for M3-X1** (which must resolve it, replace the pinned test and remove the skip) and for M3-X2.
- The fake backend's `cancel_ignored=N` ignores the first cancel only (the re-issue is honoured) and counts polls, and the simulated worker stops itself at its next start poll once the attempt is closed; the plan's C86.4 wording was amended: "stays cancelling until the 5-minute alert" is tested on the scripted provider (`TestChaos_CancelIgnored/scripted`), the fake subtest checks that the re-issue is honoured.
- Coverage on unit data alone (`go test -race -cover`): spend 95.7 %, upload 95.1 %, money 97.4 % (edge tests added), jobs was 93.2 % against the 95 % threshold. Review fix: the fault driver (`faildriver_test.go`) now also fails `rows.Close()` and makes a row unreadable (`rows.Scan` errors) in the read-path, runner, reconciler and ingest sweeps, the race-mode sweeps start each scenario at its own offset (scenarios share code, so the strides together reach more calls) and the poll scenarios are swept every third call, and `gaps_test.go` adds direct tests for stale and lease-lost writes, malformed cursors and the typed error texts.
