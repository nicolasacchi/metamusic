**Goal.** Every new render can hear itself. A take rendered after this track lands carries a listening check by default (the
operator's decision): its vocals are separated with Demucs, its words are timed by faster-whisper, its sections are measured from those
words, its diction is scored against the lyrics, and where the take has a plan score its sung melody is compared with the score's
melody. The result reaches the take page, the Ears flags ("ear checks"), the blind spots, the perception block and the agent context.
Two features stand on it: the **Recover recipe** (an imported reference with engine `unknown` becomes an editable draft from its own
transcript, linked to the reference by a `recover` mutation when rendered) and the **Italian Diction Lab** (a karaoke view with
word-level intelligibility, a personal lexicon of mangled words, human line marks that override the recogniser, a pre-spend lint
warning, and a calibration set built from new Italian takes). The existing library is not analysed; an on-demand analyze of chosen
takes exists for Recover and for the Diction Lab, always through an ordinary estimate and approval.

**Architecture (one paragraph).** Tier B is a GPU stage of the yue2 worker image (`mmears`, a subprocess next to the resident YuE2
pipeline) reached only through `mm.worker/2`: on a render it runs after the take is saved (option `options.tier_b`, on by default for
yue2 renders with lyric lines, priced on top of the render's timeout), and on a job of kind `analyze` it runs on a fetched master
(estimate item mode `analyze`). The worker writes raw outputs only — word timings, segments, a vocal pitch contour — into
`analysis.json` (`mm.ears-b/1`). Everything else is computed in Go on the server by a pure package, `internal/ears/tierb`: alignment
and diction, measured sections, coarse melody adherence against the plan score (`internal/score/abc`, the Desk track's parser), and
the Recover transcript. `internal/ears` stores the results as immutable `analyses` rows of tier `B` and `sections` rows of source
`measured`, from a take hook (renders) or a kind ingester (analyze jobs), and recomputes them from the stored artifact with a CPU-only
sweep. `internal/diction` serves the Diction Lab on read. The capability gate of G1 keeps all of it unestimable until the operator has
rolled out an image whose `ears` and `analyze` capabilities passed their canary.

**Plan decisions:**

- **D-EARS-1** Tier B runs **in the GPU job** (after the render, or as the whole of an analyze job) as a subprocess; the worker emits raw
  outputs only (words, segments, pitch contour, timings, resources); diction, alignment, measured sections, adherence and the Recover
  transcript are computed in Go on the server, deterministic, versioned and recomputable from the stored artifact (D-M5-19 kept).
- **D-EARS-2** `options.tier_b` is tri-state. **Absent (`auto`)**: on for every yue2 render take whose request has at least one lyric
  line, whose Tier B fits the runtime cap and whose routed backend has capabilities `v2` and `ears` open; otherwise off with a warning
  naming why. **`true`**: on, and a closed capability is 503 `GENERATION_DISABLED` `worker_outdated` (G1 C322.5), a take that does not
  fit is 422 `INVALID_ITEM` rule `tier_b_exceeds_runtime`, another engine is 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED` field `options.tier_b`; a take with
  no lyric line is off with a warning (nothing to hear). **`false`**: off. Transforms, canary cases without lyrics and analyze items are
  unaffected.
- **D-EARS-3** Tier B time is **added on top** of the render's timeout (D-G1-19): `ears_s = MM_EARS_LOAD_S + ⌈MM_EARS_RTF_MILLI × audio
  / 1000⌉ (+ ⌈MM_EARS_TRANSCRIBE_RTF_MILLI × audio / 1000⌉ when transcribing)`, `budget_s = ⌈3 × ears_s / 2⌉`, `timeout_s = /1 timeout +
  budget_s`, `expected_s = /1 expected + ears_s`. The worker's render window equals `/1`'s. `spend.TakeIn` gains `EarsS`; its zero value
  is byte-identical (C327.3).
- **D-EARS-4** Stages: `separate` and `asr` always; `transcribe` (vocal pitch contour) only when the take will have a plan score: the
  request carries `abc`, or its `cot` is `full`; for an analyze item, when the track has a `scores` row.
- **D-EARS-5** ASR language = `yue2.DetectLanguage(lyrics)` forced in faster-whisper; unknown (and every reference) ⇒ auto-detect. No
  prompt is ever given (prompting with the intended lyrics would hide the errors Tier B is for; D-M5-21 kept).
- **D-EARS-6** Models are **baked into the public yue2 image**, sha256-pinned in `worker/ears/models.lock.json`, MIT-licensed only:
  Demucs `htdemucs` (separation), Whisper large-v3 as a CTranslate2 conversion for faster-whisper (ASR), torchcrepe `full` (pitch).
  The lock refuses any entry whose licence is not on the allow-list (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-3-Clause). No network volume, no fetch at
  run time (D-G1-15).
- **D-EARS-7** **Score adherence is coarse melody adherence**: per section, the duration-weighted pitch-class histogram of the sung
  notes (segmented in Go from the pitch contour of the separated vocals) against the plan score's first voice (absolute and at the best
  transposition), plus the tempo ratio and key relation of Tier A's measured tempo and key against the score's `Q:` and `K:`. It is
  labelled "coarse, not note by note"; harmony is not judged. SheetSage2 is not used.
- **D-EARS-8** Measured sections come from the lyric alignment of ASR word timings (D-M5-20 rule, C335), written once per track and only
  when the track has no `measured` rows; `sections.lines_json` keeps main's string-array shape; per-line times live in the
  `tier_b_diction` row.
- **D-EARS-9** Storage: `analyses` rows of tier `B`: `tier_b_raw`, `tier_b_diction`, `tier_b_adherence`; immutable, versioned, `ON
  CONFLICT DO NOTHING`. A CPU-only sweep recomputes missing rows from stored `analysis*.json` artifacts; nothing in this track ever
  creates GPU work on its own.
- **D-EARS-10** A Tier B failure never fails a render: the take completes and its listening check reads `error`, `skipped` or `missing`.
  An analyze job whose `analysis.json` is malformed fails `WORKER_CONTRACT` (paid, valueless, visible); one whose document says
  `status: error` completes and stores it.
- **D-EARS-11** No vocal stem is kept in v1 (`keep_vocals` is always false; the `/2` field stays reserved).
- **D-EARS-12** On-demand analyze is **item mode `analyze`** of `estimate_generation` (D-G1-16): `track_ids` 1..50, analyze items alone
  in their estimate, references allowed, audio ≤ 600 s (the `jobs.expected_audio_s` CHECK), one analysis per track (no re-analysis in
  v1), never covered by a mandate, the usual approval rules (an Editor's batch always awaits the Owner).
- **D-EARS-13** **Recover = analyze + `recover_recipe`.** The GPU part is an ordinary analyze of the reference; `recover_recipe` is a
  free write that builds an editable draft from the stored transcript (lyrics in YuE2 sections, a style from the caller or a placeholder
  from the measurements) and records it in `draft_recoveries`. The reference never changes. Rendering the draft (`from_draft`) records
  the Operator Ledger mutation `recover` whose parent is the reference; the child is a new root (generation 0).
- **D-EARS-14** Diction Lab: lines are computed on read; the lexicon is computed, never stored; only human marks and lexicon overrides
  are stored. **Human marks override the recogniser**: a line's shown class is the human mark when one exists, and a line the human
  marked `clear` contributes nothing to the lexicon. Marks are `OwnerOnly` (an Owner delegate agent's marks are stored as source
  `critic` and never affect the lexicon or calibration; the M2a ratings rule).
- **D-EARS-15** `diction_risk` is a lint **warning**, never an error: a lyric word whose lexicon entry has ≥ 3 attempts, a mangled rate
  ≥ 0.5 and status `active`.
- **D-EARS-16** Timing honesty: a time is shown as measured only when it came from ASR word timings (`heard`, or `interpolated` between
  two heard neighbours); section-proportional times are labelled **estimated**, never word-level, never sent to Subsonic. No `lrc`
  basis (D-EARS-OP-2).
- **D-EARS-17** No new first-class MCP tool (D-G1-11). Every new operation is `mcp: generic`. This track's share of the program's
  4 000-byte `tools/list` budget is **1 200 bytes**: `estimate_generation` (item mode `analyze`, `track_ids`, `options.tier_b`,
  `TakePlan.tier_b`) and the Dossier's tier value `B`; all Tier B detail is reached through the generic `get_track_tier_b`.
- **D-EARS-18** U1 classification per C349.1: Tier B reads and the lexicon are scope `read` (Owner, Editor, agents); marks and lexicon
  overrides are `write` + `OwnerOnly`; `recover_recipe` is `write` (Editors may recover, as they may create drafts); Listeners reach none
  of the new operations in v1 (open question 1). No new human-only exception.
- **D-EARS-19** `/context` and `ears.Perception` gain `diction` and `adherence` **after** `energy`, present only when Tier B data exists,
  so every existing golden stays byte-identical (the L3 `key`/`energy` precedent).
- **D-EARS-20** The lexicon is keyed by `(lang, fold)`; stop words exist for Italian in v1 (`it`), other languages count every word of
  three or more runes.
- **D-EARS-21** The ears subprocess runs next to the resident YuE2 pipeline (no unload); the parent frees its CUDA cache before the spawn;
  the venv layout (shared `/opt/venv` without torchaudio, or a separate `/opt/ears` with its own torch) is decided by the `ears-image`
  spike against an image-growth budget (C333.5).
- **D-EARS-22** The ASR model is Whisper large-v3; `large-v3-turbo` is the alternative if the W2 canary or the calibration shows the
  full model too slow or no more faithful on sung Italian (a lock change plus a worker wave; open question 2).

### C334. Server analysis I: text, alignment, diction (`internal/ears/tierb`)

1. **Text.** `Norm(s)`: NFC, lowercase, `'` and `’` ⇒ space, every rune that is not a letter or digit ⇒ space, spaces collapsed (accents
   kept). `Fold(s)` = `strings.ToLower(textfold.Fold(Norm(s)))`. `RefWords(lyrics string) []RefWord` (`{Section, Line, Word int; Text,
   Norm, Fold string}`): the words of every lyric line of `yue2.LyricsSections(lyrics)`; sections without lines are instrumental.
   `TestNorm_Table` (apostrophes split elisions: `"L'amore"` ⇒ `l`, `amore`; accents kept by `Norm`, removed by `Fold`).
2. **Alignment.** `Levenshtein(a, b []rune) int`, `CharSim(a, b string) float64` (1 − Levenshtein / max runes; `""` vs `""` = 1),
   `Align(ref, hyp []string, sim func(a, b string) float64) []Pair` (Needleman-Wunsch; costs M 0, S 1 − sim, D and I 1; ties M, S, D, I;
   deterministic), with `sim = CharSim∘Fold`. `TestAlign_Table`, `FuzzAlign` (every ref and hyp index appears exactly once, order kept).
3. **Diction** `Diction(ref []RefWord, raw Raw) DictionResult`: per vocal section and per line, CER = Levenshtein(Norm ref text, Norm hyp
   text) / max(1, ref runes), WER from edit counts on `Fold` tokens; a line's hypothesis is the M/S words aligned to its words plus the
   insertions between its first and last aligned position (other insertions belong to the next line); overall values weighted by
   reference length; `mangled` = S pairs with `Fold(ref) ≠ Fold(hyp)` (≤ 200, with section, line and time). Every word outcome is kept
   per line (`{fold, op: M|S|D, heard_fold, start_s, end_s}`) for the lexicon and the karaoke.
4. **Golden** (`TestDiction_Example`): line `"la chiave del mare"` heard `la kiave del male` ⇒ `chiave` S (sim 1 − 2/6), `mare` S (1 −
   1/4), CER 3/18 = 0.1667; line `"sotto il cielo"` heard `sotto cielo` ⇒ `il` D. Also `TestDiction_PerfectASR` (all zero),
   `TestDiction_InsertionsAssigned`, `TestDiction_Deterministic`.

### C335. Measured sections

1. `MeasuredSections(ref []RefWord, secs []yue2.Section, pairs []Pair, raw Raw) ([]Measured, bool)`: a vocal section is aligned when (M +
   S with sim ≥ 0.6) / its words ≥ 0.4; start = first such word's start − 0.25 s (≥ 0), confidence = that ratio; instrumental sections fill
   the gaps (leading, between, trailing from last word end + 0.5 s), confidence 0.5; each end = the next start (last = duration). Valid
   only when ≥ half of the vocal sections aligned, starts strictly increase and every section is ≥ 1 s; otherwise none and flag
   `alignment_failed`.

   `sections` rows of source `measured` with `kind`, `label`, `raw_label` from the lyrics section, `lines_json` = the section's lines
   (main's shape), in one transaction, only when the track has no `measured` row (else a no-op).

### C336. Score adherence (coarse melody adherence; lane `ears-adherence`)

1. **Inputs**: the take's score (`scores` row and `score.abc` artifact), the parsed `pitch` contour, measured sections (else the score's
   own timing, basis `plan_timing`), Tier A tempo and key (`analyses` `tempo`, `key`) when present.
2. **Interface needed from `internal/score/abc`** (the C350s track; this lane waits for it and amends this list if its plan differs):
   `abc.Parse(src []byte) (*abc.Tune, error)`; `Tune.Meter`, `Tune.Tempo` (`Q:` as beats per minute of a stated unit, nil when absent),
   `Tune.Key` (tonic and mode from `K:`), `Tune.Voices[i].Notes []abc.Note{Bar int; Start, Dur` (exact fractions of a whole note from the
   tune start) `; MIDI int` (−1 for a rest, key signature and accidentals applied) `}`, `Tune.Sections []{Label string; StartBar, Bars
   int}` (from `%` comment lines, the semantics of `yue2.ParseScore`), `Tune.SecondsPerBar() (float64, bool)`.
3. **Sung notes** (`tierb.SungNotes(p Pitch) []Note`): voiced frames have `conf ≥ 50`; median filter over 5 frames; a note is a run of ≥ 4
   voiced frames (80 ms) within ±60 cents of the run's median; pitch = round(median / 100); weight = duration.
4. **Comparison** (`tierb.Adherence(in AdherenceInput) AdherenceResult`): score section *i* (by order) maps to measured section *i* of the
   same order (plan times scaled into the measured window); per section with ≥ 4 notes on both sides: duration-weighted pitch-class
   histograms `h_sung`, `h_plan`; `chroma = cos(h_sung, h_plan)`; `chroma_best = max over k ∈ −5..+6 of cos(rot_k h_sung, h_plan)` with
   `transpose = k`. Overall `melody_chroma` = mean of `chroma_best` weighted by plan note duration; `transpose_mode` the most frequent
   `transpose`; `tempo_ratio` = Tier A BPM / score BPM (null when either is absent); `key_relation` ∈ `exact | relative | different |
   unknown`. `basis: "coarse"`.
5. **Flags** (C338.2): `melody_off_plan` (warn: `melody_chroma < 0.5` with ≥ 3 sections compared), `sung_transposed` (info:
   `transpose_mode ≠ 0` in at least half the sections). Blind spot: "melody adherence measured coarsely (pitch classes per section, from
   the separated vocals), not note by note; harmony is not judged".
6. Tests: `TestSungNotes_FromFrames`, `TestAdherence_IdenticalMelody` (1.0, transpose 0), `TestAdherence_TransposedMelody` (a fifth up ⇒
   `chroma` < 0.5, `chroma_best` 1.0, transpose 7 → reported as −5), `TestAdherence_TempoKeyRelation` (Q 1/4=100, `K:Am` vs Tier A 104 BPM,
   C major ⇒ 1.04, `relative`), `TestAdherence_PlanTimingFallback`, `TestAdherence_TooFewNotesNull`.

## Rollout and risks

1. **Default-on cost.** Every new lyric-bearing render pays Tier B: the ceiling of a typical take grows by about a fifth and its
   expected cost by about 45 % at the illustrative price (C339.2); real figures in the private appendix. Caps are unchanged (C327), so
   fewer takes fit a batch, a day or a night. Mitigation: the estimate shows it per take; "Skip the listening check" per estimate; the
   operator can stop the default without a deploy by `runpod contract close --caps ears` (auto mode then turns Tier B off with a warning).
2. **Stranded queued renders on a capability close** (auto-close on `KIND_UNAVAILABLE`, or a manual close): renders already queued with
   `needs` `[ears, v2]` cannot be claimed and end `NOT_SUBMITTED` after 24 h (reservations released, nothing billed). Mitigation:
   amendment A3 (warn and count before a manual close; fail such jobs early with `WORKER_OUTDATED`).
3. **RTF learning would stop** once every new render has needs (G1 C323.8). Mitigation: amendment A1.
4. **Image growth and cold starts**: Whisper large-v3 alone adds about 3 GB; a second torch would add several more. Mitigation: the
   shared-venv spike and the budget (C333.5), FlashBoot, the runbook's "a long first start is not a hang"; first starts are unbilled.
   GitHub runner disk: the free-disk step (C333.4).
5. **VRAM**: YuE2 stays resident (about 11–14 GiB) while the subprocess loads its models one at a time; an OOM is recorded (`OOM`) and the
   take still completes. The W2 canary records the peak.
6. **ASR on sung Italian is uncalibrated**: diction numbers are evidence, never a gate; flags say "suspected"; the calibration (EARS-X5)
   picks thresholds from the operator's own marks; agents see the blind-spot wording.
7. **Licences**: only MIT/Apache/BSD models enter the public image (lock check); SheetSage2 is not used.
8. **Recover hallucinations** (Whisper loops, invented lines in instrumental parts): the guards of C342.1, the confidence in the answer,
   and the draft is always edited and linted by a human or agent before any render.
9. **Hot spots** this track touches (one serial PR each, merge order of C328): `internal/spend/quote.go` (Ears is the first `TakeIn`
   term), `internal/generation/{service.go (one field), estimate.go (one call), expand.go (two calls), plan.go (analyze validation,
   recovered drafts), types.go (paramsDoc.RecoveredFrom)}`, `internal/lineage/modes.go` (`EarsModes`, two operators),
   `internal/library/write.go` (operator lists, recover parent), `internal/api/{types_m3.go, types_e2.go, ops_generation_m3.go, api.go}`,
   `internal/trackctx/trackctx.go` (key order), `internal/engine/yue2/lint.go`, `internal/drafts/drafts.go` (lint context),
   `internal/provider/runpod/canary.go` (one append), `worker/Dockerfile`, `.github/workflows/worker-image.yml`, the web routing files.
