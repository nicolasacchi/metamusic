   by main; the plan's default is now **E2a = turbo only** (the ACE-Step main bundle is the endpoint's one cached model, `MM_ACESTEP_TASKS=turbo`) and the base DiT (`complete`, `stems`) is an **E2b** decision between

5. **Init, first pull and trips follow main, not the draft.** Main's reconciler decided (canary #2/#3): an INITIALIZING worker costs nothing (billing starts at container start), a first image pull took 8–12 min and a first cached-model
   download 25–46 min, so **elapsed INITIALIZING never trips** (it warns once after 30 min); `crash_loop` needs evidence (≥ 3 running→initializing restarts in 30 min, or UNHEALTHY for 5 min, or two sweeps of a long-uptime UNHEALTHY worker).
   The draft's `MM_INIT_TRIP_S`/`MM_FIRST_PULL_TRIP_S`/`MM_UNHEALTHY_TRIP_S`, `FirstPullS` quote reservation and "INITIALIZING 10 min = crash_loop" (baseline row 10 of the draft) are **dropped** (D-E2-18/19 rewritten). What stays: the worker fails fast so that a bad
   image/model produces the evidence quickly, and every rule becomes **per backend** (one `Reconciler` instance per backend, shared lease; D-E2-39).
6. **Quote contract rewritten (C152).** Main's `spend.QuoteTakes(p, []TakeIn{AudioS, Full})` already exists and is *phase-aware* (`LoadS=10`, `DefaultRTFMilli=680`, `PlanMilli=220`, `ExpectedUploadS=10`, timeout =
   `min(MaxRuntimeS, ⌈1.6·gen⌉+10+60+10)`); the draft's `QuoteTakes(p, []TakeLoad)` name collided and its formulas (`max(⌈1.5·exp⌉, exp+90)`) and goldens were wrong for main. E2 extends `TakeIn` (per-take `RTFMilli`, `LoadS`, `PlanMilli` stay) and the goldens
   are recomputed (a 480 s base-class take is **refused** at the default 900 s runtime; the bound is 410 s). `rtf_stats` (per GPU class, yue2) stays untouched; ingest must stop calling `RecordRTF` for non-yue2 takes (it would corrupt yue2's learned RTF).
7. **`min_audio_s` floor is yue2-only.** `engine.MinAudioPlanner` (optional interface; `forceMinimum` in `generation/expand.go`) exists because YuE2 can end early; ACE-Step has explicit durations, so `acestep.Engine` must **not** implement it, `TakeSpec.MinAudioS` is 0 and the worker's `min_audio_s` input key is not part of the ACE schema (C153, test `TestMinAudio_NotPlannedForACEStep`).
8. **Reference audio uses a new clip renderer (C165).** `internal/media/clip` is a 30–45 s, 100 ms-grid, loudness-matched duel clip (10 ms fades, `GainCDB ≤ 0`); the draft's `Spec{Raw, FillS}` does not exist and the clip key/validation reject 2–30 s windows. C165 adds a separate deterministic renderer with its own key, same cache.
   A/B of a repaint "at the range" also cannot use a sub-30 s duel window (duel windows are the clip window): C149.5 widens to the 30 s grid window covering the range.
9. **Suites' tape is a real pipeline change (C159.4).** `tape.Pipeline` decodes each item through a per-item `gainStage` and concatenates (gapless by construction); there is no `Plan`, `Overlaps` or `TransitionIn`. Crossfades/gaps mean a new mixing stage, new `Item` field, new total-sample arithmetic and chapter offsets, owned by one lane after L2's tape tests are re-read. T1 (C200–C249) states it does not replace these joins.

C164 writes `tracks.bpm_declared` at ingest and teaches `features` a `declared` key/tempo from `request.bpm`/`.keyscale`

**Goal:** a second render engine behind the same three faces and the same money rules. ACE-Step 1.5 (code MIT,
weights MIT per model card; one DiT + a 1.7B planner LM + VAE) renders **up to the verified per-pool maximum**
(480 s by default on 24 GB pools; 600 s only on a larger pool that passed the canary), takes a **BPM, key, time signature and explicit duration**,
**extends** a take, **repaints** a time range of a take, renders with **reference audio** for timbre continuity,
**completes** a take with named instrument tracks and **extracts stems** (extend, complete and stems are E2b) and,
off the spine, returns **LRC lyric timestamps** from a separate alignment pass (E2b). Each capability is a typed generation mode (Operator Ledger operator), estimated and
confirmed through M3's spend protocol (reservation, caps, ceiling, approvals, human 2FA path), rendered on its own
RunPod endpoint by a worker image of the same `mmworker` family, ingested through M1's frozen-master pipeline and
fingerprinted per environment. A **blind-duel engine trial** over a golden playlist decides adoption of the engine
(a human act; the numbers are an aid, an override carries a reason). A **Suite** (a Record whose movements carry
transition specs) renders movement N with movement N−1 as reference and exports as one gapless tape with
sample-exact crossfades. Worker initialisation failures fail fast and trip the reconciler **for that backend only**.

**Architecture:** extends M3's seams, adds no parallel stack.
- `internal/engine` — `Caps` v2 (adds `Extend`, `Complete`, `LRC`, `Tempo`, `Key`, `MinSeconds`, interval limits,
  stem names); `Engine.Artifacts` stays per engine. `internal/engine/acestep` (new) — `Request`, `Validate`,
  `FromYuE2`, `ExpectedAudioSeconds` (exact, never clamped), artifacts, pinned weights, the **verified-features
  file** that turns capabilities on only after a canary proved them (D-E2-7). `engine/registry` registers it iff
  `MM_ACESTEP=1`.
- `internal/lineage` — mode table gains an engine axis and seven rows (`port`, `extend`, `repaint`, `reference`,
  `complete`, `stems`, plus ACE patch fields on the four existing variant modes); Operator Ledger operators
  `engine_port`, `extend`, `repaint`, `reference_render`, `complete_tracks`, `extract_stems`.
- `internal/generation` — expansion of the new items (source/reference resolution, per-engine validation, one
  engine per batch), estimate warnings, `options.trial_id`.
- `internal/spend` — `QuoteTakes` over the extended `TakeIn` (per-take `RTFMilli`, `LoadS`); `engine_rtf` keyed by
  `(engine, task_class, gpu_class)`; per-backend cold start. Caps, ledger, approvals and tokens are unchanged and
  global.
- `internal/provider` — **`Set`**: ordered backends, `Route(engine, requested)`, per-backend `ColdStartS()`,
  `TakeSpec.Inputs` (signed fetch URLs minted at submit). `provider/fake` gains `fake-acestep` (SimWorker branch
  per engine); `provider/runpod` gains an `EngineProfile` (yue2 unchanged, acestep15 new), per-engine provisioning (one cached model per endpoint), a two-endpoint probe and canary cases.
- `internal/jobs` — runner iterates backends, one reconciler per backend; main's init-failure evidence rules per backend (C151.4);
  ingest per engine (`truncated` only for yue2; stem artifacts and `lyrics.lrc`, both E2b).
- `internal/enginetrial` (new) — golden playlist → port items → blind duels → tally → decision. `internal/suites`
  (new) — Suites over L2 Records, `suite_movement` estimate items, transition-aware tape plans.
- `worker/` — `mmworker.models` (explicit snapshot resolution for N pinned models, checkpoint layout, fail-fast
  exit), `mmworker.engines.{yue2,acestep}` adapters selected by `ENGINE`, `mmworker.inputs` (verified fetch),
  `Dockerfile.acestep` (own torch/transformers pins, no weights), per-engine JSON schemas, contract job.

- The SPA gains an engine picker and mode panels on `/human/generate`, range selection on the waveform, track
  actions (Extend, Repaint, Reference, Stems, Complete), `/engines`, `/engines/trials/:id`, `/suites/:id`.

## Decisions recorded by this plan

These refine the design of record; E2-X1 copies them into the private design document. **Rows marked (rev 3) were rewritten or added against `main` 5f6eeaf; the others are the draft's text.**

| Id | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D-E2-1 | **One serverless endpoint per engine** (`mm-yue2`, `mm-acestep`), one image per engine, one backend per endpoint (`runpod-serverless`, `runpod-serverless-acestep`), each with its own `provider_state`, quote, trips, sweep, billing source, meter and cold-start allowance. **Revisit only if** the canary shows the two images can share a torch/transformers pin set *and* the model set fits one 24 GB card *and* cold starts are within 20 % — none of which is expected. | ACE-Step's torch/transformers pins conflict with `yue2-infer`'s; the cached-model sets differ; a trip, crash loop or drift on one engine must not stop the other; `workers.max`, `queueDelay` and the cold start differ; a shared endpoint would force the larger cold start onto every YuE2 take. |
| D-E2-2 | **Same worker package, engine adapters.** `mmworker` stays one package; `ENGINE` (default `yue2`) selects the adapter once at process start; a job whose `engine` differs is `BAD_INPUT`. The contract string stays **`mm.worker/1`** and a yue2 job is **byte-identical** to M3's (no `engine`, no `inputs` key); ACE-Step jobs add an optional `engine` discriminator, optional `inputs` and engine-specific `request`/schema selected by it. | M5 reserves `/2` for Tier B; a discriminator keeps the M3 wire compatible, so the yue2 image and every M3 test keep working unchanged, and an old yue2 image can never receive an ACE-Step job (routing is by engine). |
| D-E2-3 | **Model families by task class.** `turbo` class = `text2music`, `repaint`, `extend`, `reference` (turbo DiT, 8 steps, no CFG, 1.7B LM planner for text2music). `base` class = `complete`, `stems` (base DiT, 50 steps, CFG; ACE-Step documents extract/lego/complete as base-model-only). RTF, fixed overhead and timeouts are per class. | The documented capability matrix; the quote must use the RTF of the model that renders. |
| D-E2-4 (rev 3) | **Memory budget is measured, written down and enforced at preflight; E2a ships `turbo` only.** The turbo task class needs the main bundle only (VAE, Qwen3 text encoder, turbo DiT, 1.7B LM) = the endpoint's **one cached model** (D-E2-5, D-E2-34). The budget is `turbo DiT + 1.7B LM weights + Qwen3 encoder + VAE + vllm KV-cache reservation + DiT/VAE activation peak at the pool's maximum duration` (the reservation is the LM backend's `gpu_memory_utilization`, pinned in `engine_params.json`, D-E2-31; 480 s on 24 GB, D-E2-14); E2-P0b measures every term on the pinned stack and commits `worker/src/mmworker/engines/acestep_memory_budget.json` (raw numbers stay in private notes); the rule is `Σ ≤ 0.90 × pool VRAM`, evaluated by the worker at preflight (`NO_MEMORY`, fail fast, C154.5). **Until P0b/X4a measured it the file says `"measured": false` and the preflight only logs** (never silently passes a number it does not have). For E2b, a `turbo,base` load adds the base DiT (≈ 4.7 GB) and needs a 32/48 GB pool unless it is measured to fit 24 GB; an endpoint provisioned with `turbo` only never loads or needs the base model. The earlier "≈ 4.7 GB × 2" figure is **not** a budget. | Eager loading keeps one `ColdStartS` per backend and fails fast at init instead of mid-job; the LM's vllm reservation and the activation peak grow with duration and are what exhaust a card; an unmeasured budget must say so. |
| D-E2-5 (rev 3) | **One cached model per endpoint is the baseline fact; the second model is a build decision, not an assumption.** Main proved it (M3 D-M3-18/38): RunPod offers exactly one cached model per endpoint and the REST v2 snapshot has no cached-model field (`consoleSteps`). **C159.4 Tape (amends L2 C128; `export_record_tape`'s API does not change; D-E2-21).** What main has (`internal/tape`): `Item`/`Snapshot`/`Job`/`PlannedItem`, `PlanGains`, a pipeline that decodes **each item through its own `gainStage`** (24-bit integer gain, `ErrClipped` on a clamp) into **one FLAC encoder pipe**, per-item frame counts → `PlanChapters(items, frames)` (chapter i starts at `Σ_{j<i} n_j`) → `TotalSamples(chapters)`, and `verifyFinal` (`TAPE_SAMPLE_MISMATCH`); `encodeArgs(out, estimateSamples(items))` hands the encoder the exact input size. - A **`joinStage`** between each item's `gainStage` and the encoder pipe holds the last `k` frames of the previous item (not yet written) and, when the next item arrives, writes `mix(tail, head)` with equal-power gains `cos(θ), sin(θ)`, `θ = π/2 · (j + 0.5)/k`, from a precomputed Q30 table, over 24-bit values; `gap g` writes `g` zero frames; `gapless` is today's behaviour. A mixed sample outside 24 bits ⇒ `TAPE_CLIPPED` exactly as the gain stage. `k` ≤ ⌊min(n_{i−1}, n_i)/2⌋ else 422 `TRANSITION_TOO_LONG` **before anything is started** (the length is known from the snapshot's `DurationS` and re-checked against the real frame counts).
- Arithmetic: total samples `Σ n − Σ k + Σ g`; `estimateSamples` returns it (the encoder is told the exact size, as today); chapter i starts at `Σ_{j<i} n_j − Σ_{j≤i} k_j + Σ_{j≤i} g_j` (its own first sample; neighbouring chapters overlap by `k`); `TotalSamples` stays "last start + last length" and equals the encoded total, so `verifyFinal` is unchanged; `CuesheetOf` offsets stay strictly increasing (a crossfade of `k ≥ 1` with `n ≥ 2k` cannot reorder them).

### C163. Engine-neutral recipe seam (NEW; E2a lane F1; D-E2-33)

**Why.** On main every recipe path is typed `yue2`: `library.Service.InsertRecipe(ctx, tx, p yue2.Parsed, createdBy)` writes `engine = 'yue2'`; `lineage.Apply/ApplyParsed(base yue2.Request, op, params, patch)`, `RequestOf`, `ParseRequest` and the aliases `Request = yue2.Request`, `Issue`, `ParseError`, `MaxSeed` are yue2; `lineage.ReusableDraftRecipe` requires `engine == "yue2"`; `generation/plan.go` (`DefaultEngine`, calls at the three `RequestOf/ParseRequest` sites) and `estimate.go:cotIsFull` read yue2 JSON; the Runner already resolves `Engines[j.Engine]` for artifacts only. Without a seam the mode table of C149 cannot be implemented.
**Contract** (`internal/engine/recipe.go`; the **names below are the intent, F1 re-reads `lineage`/`generation` and records any rename here in the same PR**):
```go
type Recipe struct {                       // the engine-neutral facts the library, lineage and generation read
	ID, Style, Lyrics, Cot string          // Style = yue2 style | acestep caption; Cot = yue2 cot | acestep "think"|"off"
	Seed                   int64
	Canonical              []byte          // canonical engine-native request (recipes.request_json)
	Hash, CoreHash         string          // C148.5
	Warnings               []Issue
}
type RecipeCodec interface {               // optional capability of engine.Engine (type-asserted like MinAudioPlanner)
	ParseRecipe(raw json.RawMessage) (Recipe, []Issue)                                  // strict; canonical round trip is the identity
	WithSeedAndID(base Recipe, seed int64, id string) (Recipe, error)
	ApplyMode(base Recipe, mode string, patch json.RawMessage, p ModeParams) (Recipe, error) // engine-native patch rules; yue2 delegates to lineage.Apply
}
```
`yue2.Engine` implements it by delegating to the existing functions (**byte-identical**: `TestRecipeCodec_Yue2ByteIdentical` replays every M3 estimate/ingest golden and the L1–L3 fixtures through the seam and compares canonical bytes, hashes and `recipe_keys`), `acestep.Engine` implements it natively (C148). `library.InsertRecipe` becomes `InsertRecipe(ctx, tx, engine string, r engine.Recipe, createdBy)` (the old `yue2.Parsed` entry point stays as a thin wrapper for the importer and drafts); `lineage.ReusableDraftRecipe` stays yue2 (drafts are yue2); `generation` plans through the registry's codec chosen by the item's engine and keeps `DefaultEngine = "yue2"`. **No `if engine == "acestep15"` outside `internal/engine/**` and the two ingest bridges (C156, C164).** The yue2-only paths — drafts, `lint_recipe`, import, `songs.jsonl` — keep calling `yue2` directly.

### C165. Reference clips: `clip.Service.Reference` (NEW; E2a lane G2; D-E2-9, D-E2-38)

`internal/media/clip` renders duel clips only (`Def "mm-clip/1"`, `OutRate 48000`, `FadeMS 10`, `GridMS 100`, `MinClipMS 30000`, `MaxClipMS 45000`, loudness gains via `GainCDB`). The draft's `Spec{Raw, FillS}` does not exist and `validate` rejects 2–30 s windows. E2 adds a **separate renderer in the same package and cache** (no change to duel keys or goldens):
```go
const RefDef = "mm-refclip/1"; const RefMS = 30000                 // the clip is ALWAYS exactly 30.000 s = 1 440 000 frames at 48 kHz
type RefSpec struct{ MasterSHA256, MasterPath string; SampleRate, Channels int; FromMS, ToMS int64 }   // window 2..30 s on the 100 ms grid, inside the master
func (s *Service) Reference(ctx context.Context, sp RefSpec) (Object, error)
func RefKey(sp RefSpec, toolVersion string) string                 // sha256 over RefDef ‖ master sha ‖ from ‖ to ‖ rate ‖ channels ‖ toolVersion ‖ tile rule version; different prefix from Key()
```
Rules: cut `[from, to)` from the frozen master **raw** (unity gain, no loudness match, no fades), **tile (loop) the window end to end until exactly 1 440 000 frames**, output 48 kHz, channels = source (1 or 2), FLAC into `clips/<key[:2]>/<key>.flac` (same directory, eviction and singleflight as duel clips; `Resolve` serves it through the signed `/media/` route like any clip). The object's sha256 is the **`clip_sha256`** pinned in the recipe. Estimate resolves the object and freezes `{track_id, clip_sha256, from_ds, to_ds}`; **create/submit re-ensures it and compares the sha** — a different sha (tool upgrade) ⇒ 409 `ESTIMATE_STALE` reason `reference_changed`; the worker asserts the fetched file is 30.000 s and sha-matches (C154.3). Defaults: the last 20 s of the take (`from = max(0, dur − 20 s)`); a window shorter than 2 s or longer than 30 s ⇒ `INVALID_ITEM reference_window`; a master that is not 44.1/48 kHz mono/stereo ⇒ `ENGINE_CAPABILITY src_format`.
Tests (media tier, need ffmpeg/flac: `testtools.Require`; plus pure key tests): `TestReference_Exactly30s_Table` (windows 2.0, 2.5, 12.0, 29.9, 30.0 s ⇒ every file 1 440 000 frames, flac `--test` clean), `TestReference_Deterministic` (same spec twice ⇒ same sha), `TestReference_KeyNeverCollidesWithDuelKey`, `TestReference_CacheHitAndEviction`, `TestReference_DefaultTail20s` (track 100.0 s ⇒ from 800 ds, to 1000 ds; 12.0 s ⇒ 0..120 ds), `TestReference_ToolVersionChangesKey`; main's clip suites pass **unmodified**.

### C166. Non-take job outputs: stems and LRC (NEW; **E2b**; D-E2-11, D-E2-22, D-E2-36)

Main's ingest ends every completed job in a new `tracks` row (`jobs.track_id`, `outcome`, `env_id`). E2b adds one more terminal shape, **attach**, selected by the job's operator (`extract_stems`) and implemented in `internal/jobs/ingest_attach.go`:
- the rendered `audio.flac` is verified (digest, FLAC, 48 kHz, length = source ±0.1 s) and attached to the **source take** as artifact `stem-<name>.flac` (`artifacts(track_id, name, sha256, bytes, path)`, PK `(track_id, name)`, immutable by trigger; a repeated stem name becomes `stem-<name>-<n>.flac`, the estimate warned `stem_exists`; names are safe top-level names per M1 C16.1);

- `lyrics.lrc` (E2b LRC pass) is attached to the **new take** of a normal text2music job as artifact `lyrics.lrc` (≤ 1 MiB, UTF-8, every line `^\[\d{2,}:\d{2}\.\d{2,3}\]`; invalid LRC is dropped with a job event, never fails the take).
**First step of lane G3 (the C157.3 gate):** read the three consumers of `jobs.track_id` — `jobs/service.go` list filter (`source_track_id = ? OR track_id = ?`), `provenance/reconcile.go:191` (`SELECT batch_id FROM jobs WHERE track_id = ?`: the stem batch would join the parent's provenance session; decide whether that is wanted), the ingest/draft-fulfilment code — and record the result here; if any cannot cope, stems leave E2 for the C157-alt plan. Tests: `TestIngest_StemArtifact`, `TestIngest_StemArtifact_AddsNoTrackRow`, `TestStemArtifact_NoSongOrPickEffect`, `TestStemJob_ProvenanceUnchanged`, `TestIngest_LRC_Stored`, `TestIngest_LRC_InvalidDropped`, `TestGetTrack_ListsStems`.

## Risks / open questions

- **One cached model per endpoint** (D-E2-5, D-E2-34): E2a is turbo-only on it; E2b's base-class modes need the base DiT baked into the image (+≈ 4.7 GB, larger first pull, 14 GB bound) or stay off. The weekly drift job watches the REST snapshot for a model field.
- **Fresh-host model download and image pull** are unbilled but long (main measured 8–12 min and 25–46 min for yue2's smaller assets); the ACE-Step bundle and image are larger. The risk is operational (attempt TTL, queue-expiry resubmits `≤ 6`, the 30 min warning) and is measured at X4a; it is not a money risk (D-E2-18).
- **Engine seam** (C163): the largest unplanned change; guarded by byte-identical yue2 goldens; if F1a cannot keep them identical the lane stops (no yue2 behaviour change is acceptable).
- **L3 recompute/features** (C164) touch `features/load.go`: yue2 digests must not move; ACE takes without a declared key show up on the Atlas by measured key only.
- **Extend semantics** (D-E2-8) are an inference from the repaint API, not a documented feature; the canary decides. The Suite does not depend on it.
- **ACE-Step repaint rewrites the whole waveform through the VAE**, so regions outside the range are close to, not bit-identical with, the source; the canary *records* the outside similarity, the A/B action lets the operator hear it, and no promise of "untouched elsewhere" is made in the UI copy.
- **RTF seeds are unsourced placeholders** (turbo 150/120/300 milli, `LoadS` 10; base 1200/900/2400, `LoadS` 20); too low means `meter_exceeds_reserved` trips (detected, not prevented, as M3); a base-class take over 410 s is refused at the default runtime by design (D-E2-17b; fix `MM_ACESTEP_MAX_RUNTIME`).
- **Eager load of the LM + DiT + the vllm KV cache** may not fit a 24 GB pool at 480 s (the D-E2-4 budget, measured in P0b or X4a; `NO_MEMORY` fails fast); default pools stay 24 GB classes; a failure to load is `LOAD_ERROR` → fail fast → evidence → trip, not a hang.
- **Language quality** (Italian) and vocal quality are exactly what the engine trial measures; E2 makes no claim and ships no automatic ranking.
- **Reference-audio semantics** (timbre vs style, window length) are unproven; `reference` stays off in the verified file until the canary and X6 show it helps; reference renders are **not** claimed reproducible (D-E2-9).
- **Seed reproducibility** is unproven on the pinned stack (P0b item 7); `same_seed_edit` is listed only when `seed_reproducible` is true.
- **Tape transitions** (C159.4) change a shipped pipeline: non-suite Records must stay byte-identical (golden); a clipped mix fails `TAPE_CLIPPED` like the gain stage.
- **Stems as artifacts** touch no earlier package but depend on `jobs.track_id` consumers accepting the source take (C166 gate); otherwise stems leave E2 for the C157-alt plan.
- **`tools/list` growth**: none by default (D-E2-35); the ceiling is 108 300 B against 108 228 B measured.
- **Spend with two backends** multiplies settlement/billing paths (two billing sources, one cap): `TestProperty_SpendNeverExceedsCap_TwoEngines` and `TestChaos_TripOneBackendOnly` are the guards.
- **Trial candidates land beside golden takes in the same Song** (`port` children): requiring human-rated golden tracks keeps Picks stable (Pick = human stars, then newest, C158.1); a trial's takes can be discarded afterwards.
- **Per-pool duration** (D-E2-14): 24 GB tier = 480 s documented; 600 s needs a larger pool and a passing `t2m-long`.
- **Upstream defaults drift** (D-E2-31) and **image dependencies / licences** (C154.6, D-E2-29): as in the draft; the notices check can fail the image build.
- **E2b flag discipline:** a PR that flips a capability flag without the E2-X4b result is rejected in review (D-E2-7).
- **REST v1 retirement (2026-11-15):** no repo code uses v1; a v1-targeting operator tool or doc example is the only exposure (D-E2-41).


## Revision log

### Revision 3 (2026-11-06) — as shipped: what each lane changed versus the plan above

The contracts above are the design of record; where the code differs, this log says how. E2a and E2b are built; **E2c (engine trials and Suites) is not built** and is not part of this release: no trial or suite operation exists, and `engine_adoption_log` is written only by the operator CLI. Registry and `tools/list`: `list_engines` is the one new operation and it is generic; `tools/list` grew from 110,456 to 115,227 bytes (and by 78 more bytes with `JobTimings.extra`) from additive fields on seven existing tools, 39 tools, no dedicated E2 tool.

**Operator decisions.** E2a and E2b ship together; the base DiT is baked into the ACE-Step worker image because RunPod offers one cached model per endpoint (`models_mode = bundle_cached_base_baked`); adoption gating stays (an engine other than yue2 gets money only after `metamusic engines adopt`); the GPU measurements of the P0b spike are folded into the first canary; the image licences were accepted (pytorch-wavelets allowed, its DWT code being MIT; paramiko LGPL-2.1 and frozendict LGPL-3.0 used as unmodified PyPI libraries; Qwen3 Apache-2.0 credited in the image NOTICE).

**Numbering.** Migration `0015_engines` (jobs rebuild with the 600 s `expected_audio_s` CHECK, `engine_rtf`, append-only `engine_adoption_log` and its view) and **`0016_billing_backend`**, which was not in the plan (see "Per-backend billing" below). `0013` is M5's and `0014` is T1's; the plan's trials/suites migration has no number yet. Migration `0015` is generated by `scripts/gen-jobs-ddl.go` from the head schema, and `TestMigration_JobsRebuild_GeneratorCurrent` fails if a later migration touches `jobs` without regenerating it.

**Deviations from the contracts.**

- **Compose via `recipe`.** ACE-Step `compose_new` carries its full `acestep15` request in the frozen `ItemIn.recipe` object with `engine: acestep15` on the item. The plan's separate `ItemIn.Request` field was never frozen and is not needed. The compose UI is yue2-only; ACE compose is reachable through the API and MCP.
- **Reference minimum.** The ACE-Step reference minimum (`refMinDS`) was 200 deciseconds in the code against a 2 s window floor in the clip contract; the warning `reference_window_short` stayed dead until the fix. Reference clips are 2 to 30 s on a 100 ms grid, cut raw, resampled to 48 kHz, tiled to exactly 1,440,000 frames and encoded as 24-bit FLAC; the default window is the last 20 s of the source. Refusals happen before submit: `CLIP_REFERENCE_WINDOW` is `INVALID_ITEM` rule `reference_window`, `CLIP_REFERENCE_SILENT` is rule `reference_silent`, unsupported rate or channels are `ENGINE_CAPABILITY` `src_format`.
- **Bug found on the way: reference renders.** The input minter and the media route resolved the reference clip by digest while the cache is keyed by clip key, so no reference render could run (`SUBMIT_REJECTED`); a resolver now maps the digest back through the recipe.
- **Per-backend billing (migration `0016`).** The billing windows, billing buckets, worker meter and ledger had no backend column, so peer backends could only release by residual hold. The column (default `runpod-serverless`) makes each backend reconcile by hourly buckets against its own billing source; this is required before any real ACE-Step spend. `TestUpgrade_BillingBackend_FromEveryPriorSchema` covers upgrades from every earlier schema.
- **The meter fix.** The M3 worker-lifetime meter was never wired in production (`Runner.UseMeter` was not called), so since M3 every batch settled its worker overhead at zero; with readable billing the reconciler trued the ledger up after the billing lag, with no billing the overhead was never recorded. The meter is now wired (`MeterRouter` with several backends); when no lifetime is attributed the overhead falls back to the idle allowance plus 60 s after the last attempt, and it is capped at the batch's remaining overhead allowance.
- **Per-backend runner.** One runner holds one view per backend sharing the lease, the ingest pool and the clocks; the first backend drives reconciliation of the others; every batch, attempt and worker query is scoped by backend, and with a single backend the M3 path is byte-identical. A trip, a crash loop or an init failure on one backend never touches the other. `get_provider_status` reports per-backend state and the global bounds `G` (burn rate if every endpoint runs flat out) and `X` (worst simultaneous exposure); arming is refused with `CONCURRENCY_EXCEEDS_CAP` when `X` exceeds the per-batch ceiling or the month headroom (disarming is never refused).
- **Adoption.** `metamusic engines list|adopt|reject --reason` is the only adoption path (no REST or MCP route); each decision appends one log row and one audit row in one transaction; the actor is `system:cli` and the reason is the audit. yue2 is always admitted; others are admitted when the latest row is `adopted`. `ENGINE_NOT_ADOPTED` carries the CLI line as its hint, and the adoption gate fails closed when it is not wired.
- **Ingest policy.** Truncation reporting and RTF learning are per engine (`IngestPolicy`): yue2 reports truncation and learns RTF, ACE-Step does neither for `truncated` (it must be null) and learns RTF net of load (repaint and extend never update it). `tracks.bpm_declared` is written from `request.bpm`.
- **Timings.** The P0b numbers (peak VRAM per component, resident VRAM, LM memory utilisation, phase seconds) travel in `JobTimings.extra`, an additive numeric map on the job view, and the canary reads it from there.
- **Stems and LRC (E2b).** A finished `extract` job attaches `stem-<name>.flac` to the source take as an artifact (numbered from 2 for a repeat); it adds no track row, mutation, Song or Pick, and the job completes with the source take as `track_id`. A `lyrics.lrc` that is not UTF-8, larger than 1 MiB or without `[mm:ss.xx]` line starts is dropped with an `lrc_dropped` event and never fails the take. `get_media_links` carries `stems[]` and `lrc`; the media route gained the `lrc` extension. A stem job has no mutation row. The three readers of `jobs.track_id` were checked (C157.3) and cope, so stems stay in E2.
- **Job codes.** `INPUT_FAILED`, `INPUT_MISMATCH` and `TASK_UNAVAILABLE` are job terminal codes, engine-neutral; before the fix they ended as `WORKER_LOST`.
- **Worker.** Python 3.11, torch 2.10 CUDA 12.8, ACE-Step pinned at v0.1.8; the image is 13.75 GB against the 14 GB bound because ffmpeg is left out (ACE-Step uses it only for lossy formats, which are never requested). Each DiT checkpoint directory is a real directory. `extend` is a turbo repaint over appended silence (`lego` is not offered). Cancel is honoured in the LM phase, at phase boundaries and after the render, not inside the denoise loop. Repaint knobs (mode, strength, crossfade) are unpinned and stay canary candidates. A wrong sample rate is `PIPELINE_ERROR`.
- **Web.** Engine picker (shown only for an ACE intent or "Change engine"), estimate card with task class, range and reference, `RangeSelect` (drag, 44 px handles, numeric fields, arrow keys 0.1 s and shift 1 s), Repaint and "Render with this as reference" on Track, A/B of a repaint with its parent at the repaint range, Extend, Complete and Extract stems (enabled by the engine's capabilities), a stems player on the shared audio context, a synced LRC list, lineage badges and a dashed reference edge, and a read-only `/engines` capability matrix. Stem checkboxes use a fixed 12-name list, not `caps.stem_names`. A port action on Track does not exist yet.
- **Coverage and CI.** Floors: `engineadopt` 82, `provider/fake` 90 (measured). The all-capabilities features seam used by the e2e specs (`MM_E2E=1`) is refused unless every backend is fake.

**Not built / open.** E2c (trials, Suites, tape transitions); ACE compose UI; a port entry on Track; the canary has not run, so every flag in `features.verified.json` stays off and the UI says "not yet verified"; seed reproducibility, the memory budget and the real RTFs are unmeasured until the first canary; the repaint outside-range similarity is not computed offline.
